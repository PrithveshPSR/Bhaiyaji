Privacy Policy for Bhaiyaji

Last updated: July 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your information in the Bhaiyaji application.

1. Information We Collect Automatically

Google Account Data: If you choose to log in using Google Sign-In, we collect your Name, Email address, and Profile Picture URL. This is used solely to personalize your experience (e.g., "Namaste Prithvesh") and to provide cloud backup services.

Usage Data: We use Firebase Analytics to collect basic anonymous data (such as which features are used most) to help us improve the application.

2. Permissions and Media

Camera & Microphone: Our app requires access to your camera and microphone for the "Take Photo" and "Record Video" features. Media captured is stored locally in your phone's gallery. We do not transmit this media to our servers.

Storage: The app requires permission to save photos and videos to your device's public gallery.

3. Data Storage and Cloud Sync

We use Firebase Firestore to securely backup your "Bookmarked Vaanis" to the cloud. This allows you to restore your favorite sayings if you change devices or reinstall the app. This data is private and only accessible by you when logged into your account.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following Google services which may collect data in accordance with their own policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
